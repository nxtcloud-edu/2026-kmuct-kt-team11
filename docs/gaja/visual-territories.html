Gaja — visual territories

Three directions that would produce different buildings, not three tints of one idea. Pick one, or blend (“B, but A’s data register”).

Feels like a friend who actually checked — and shows the receipts. Never a travel app’s aspirational feed, or an AI product’s tech costume. Tension: committed but auditable — it makes the call and shows its work. Without the logo: every claim carries its receipt, set in a register plainer than the claim.
